# Learning culture ideas
semi unstructured musings on creating a powerful and impactful learning culture in the workplace

## What is in scope
- Career pathing
- Mentorship
- Day-to-day
- Longterm visioned
- Shareouts

## Concepts
- Reverse mentoring: pair long term individuals with new to learn from new perspective
- Internal mobility programs (rotations, gigs, inner consulting)
- Employee resource groups (working groups, support communities)
- Cross platform/role mentorships, expand circle of reach for perspective and skill building
- 1:1 coffee chats, short term mentorships, get to know you meetings
- More support for mentorship like toolkits, goal planning, agreements, proposals, weekly tasks, how to get the most out of, maximize mentorship
- Connect vs grow: connect long term growth, grow more immediate
- Goal-based learning but not with SMART goals, is there another framework? What about using Bloom's taxonomy to move across columns via verb upskilling?, keep it tied to world of education
- Internal MasterClass? best in class, rugby collegiate colors?
- Back to school celebration?
- Re: book fair?
- Mentorship fair, including pledge booth with high profile people with raffles on 1:1 coffee chat on different subjects/topics
- Customized recognition cards focused on learning, partner with designers
- Playbooks and placemats for maximizing impact, workshop prep materials, shareout slide templates
- User testing / DIY resources to learn more through data and connections
- Learning playlists from high profile people
- On-site library stocked with books for analog learning, could have more appetite to do
- Actual magazine for around campus, focus on learning or curation of topical insights


## Further reflection
- What problem are we trying to solve? As a company? What about as individual learners? Where is the overlap?
- What is great about current learning culture? How can we continue to nurture that?
- How far are we from ideal state? Are certain dimensions further away than others?
- How does this relate to other culture initiatives like engagement/involvement, job satisfaction, retention, recognition, AI/tech tooling, etc.?
- How can we add in fun? Novelty? Whimsy?
- This also depends on ability to feel comfortable talking about good AND bad outcomes of experiments, we learn from both
- How do we promote learning in short and long term? moment vs movement
- How can we encourage learning with a vision? Balance with learning by following passion?
- How can we increase positive modeling of learning? leaders? examples?
- What studies are there related to this? Gallup?
- How can we represent different ways learning shows up? learn x experience x lead
- How can we remind to learn? Think Duolingo reminders, TED Talk, streaks for things?
- Balance between informal and formal learning moments - support both
- Tab ask samsumg learning commercial reference
- Apprenticeship patterns book
- How does the ideal learning culture show up in the day to day? big moments? processes/workflows?
- Budget considerations: there are support options that require $$$ and ones that require time (mentorship, enablement worksheets, etc.)
- How can we curate from other learning programs at other companies that champion certain portions of the culture vision we have? Grab only what pushes our vision forward
