# Corporate whimsy
How do we make the 9-5 enjoyable, whimsical, and rejuvenating to foster engagement, connection, and joy?

## Brainstorming
Concepts to play with 

### Gathering for career advice, scavenger hunt
- everyone bring best pieces of career advice, swap
- find someone who ___ and swap advice with them
- at end, get all together and collage them into like mood boards


### Hot Ones -esque leadership interview
- Have leaders doing something with host while panel


### Primp my work bag/space/computer
- swap art, desk ornaments, stickers, post-its


### Mentorship fair
- Submit 1 slide GTKY, inlcude if open for mentorship or looking for mentorship
- put up on screens on boards to walk by
- enable connections
- music, cookies
- name 10 people for entering some prize

### Look club
- book club but with videos curated into theme/series
- built in learning


### Book fair
- Why this book changed my life lightning talk
- Smelly erasers, fun pens, etc.


### Async
- Desktop wallpapers, seasonal collections
- Slack schedule of questions, focus on questions from places like The Artist's Way
