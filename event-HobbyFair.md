# Event: Hobby Fair 🎪

**Objective:** allow people to bring their authentic selves to work

_How can we better challenge ourselves to do this? Step 1: talk about it_

## What this looks like
- Booths, with or without attendant
- Props people bring in to showcase self outside of work
  - Frames
  - Binders
  - Photo albums
  - Demo
  - Pics or videos to share
  - Playlist
  - Movie list
  - Recipes
- Can be one hobby or multiple, new or advanced or even just interested in
- Share:
  - How you started?
  - What you love about it?
  - Advice for beginners
  - What's it like?
  - What's a related dream?
  - What does it take to get into it?
  - Timeline
  - Wins/fails
  - What you have learned
  - How it relates to how you show up at work
- Grab a hobby wall
  - Display of envelopes including little slips of how-to papers
  - What, difficulty, price, time, portability, messiness, contact, where to start
  - Have people submit these ahead of time
- Decorate your desk free art library
  - Photography, painting, illustration, plants, recipes, etc.
  - Vintage company ads
  - Desktop wallpapers
- Plug for company hobby, fun channels and groups
- Whiteboard for engagment prompt
- Small craft station
  - Collage, paper layering, those bead lizard keychains, cool knots paracord, draw repeatable pattern, punch card DIY, DIY paper fortunes, paper manifest cards, origami, zines

### Materials
- Your name / contact fold card for if not at booth
- Tables
- Playlist
- Reserved room
- Craft station materials (dependent on decision)
- Designated area for free art library
